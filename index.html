Our story

A neighbourhood café, from the first coffee

Tucked into a quiet Georgian terrace between Angel and Regent's Canal.

Generous breakfasts, brunch and good coffee every day.

  • Breakfast all morning

    Full English, halal and vegan breakfasts on sourdough toast.

  • Brunch with a twist

    Eggs Benedict and Royale, menemen, çılbır and shakshuka.

  • Coffee & smoothies

    Flat whites, matcha and chai lattes, milkshakes and fresh juices.
